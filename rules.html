Campaign Mode — Planechase + Bosses

Complete Guide to the Gameplay System

📜 Introduction

This game mode combines Commander, Planechase, and a system of scaling bosses to create a campaign-like experience, similar to a cooperative roguelite where the Multiverse reacts to the players’ actions.

🧭 Campaign Structure

A typical campaign contains 5 to 7 planes, ending with a final boss.

  • 1. Starting Plane — low difficulty
  • 2. Wild Plane — random
  • 3. Thematic Plane — tied to the story
  • 4. Crisis Plane — stronger boss
  • 5. Twist Plane — global negative effect
  • 6. Final Plane — mythic boss

🐉 Boss Encounters

Whenever the plane changes, a boss appears associated with that plane. The boss remains until defeated or until the plane changes again.

✔ Key Rules

  • Only players who deal at least 1 damage to the boss receive rewards.
  • If the boss escapes, it applies a persistent penalty for the rest of the campaign.
  • Bosses scale based on the number of players.
  • Bosses do NOT attack players directly unless they create or control creatures. Those creatures attack during that player’s upkeep.

💎 Rewards

Players who damage the boss choose a reward based on its category:

⭐ Minor Rewards

  • Create 2 Treasures
  • Scry 3, then draw 1
  • Two +1/+1 counters
  • Look at 5 cards, keep 1

🔥 Major Rewards

  • Your next spell costs 3 less
  • Cast a spell with MV ≤3 from your graveyard
  • Create a 4/4 Angel with flying
  • Search for a basic land and put it onto the battlefield

💠 Mythic Rewards

  • Emblem: “Once per turn, add one mana of any color.”
  • Emblem: “Your first creature spell each turn costs 1 less.”
  • Emblem: “Whenever you attack a boss, draw a card.”

💀 Persistent Penalties

If a boss escapes, its penalty becomes permanent.

⚠ Minor Penalties

  • Players start with 38 life
  • Creatures enter tapped
  • At each upkeep, you lose 1 life

🔥 Major Penalties

  • Noncreature spells cost 1 more
  • Sacrifice a creature at the start of each game
  • Draw one fewer card on your first turn

💀 Mythic Penalties

  • All players start with a curse emblem
  • You cannot gain life
  • You start each game with an enemy 4/4 on the battlefield

🌍 Bosses by Plane

Each plane has a unique boss with:

  • Life scaled by number of players
  • Attack trigger
  • Chaos trigger
  • Thematic reward
  • Persistent penalty

Example — Innistrad

“The Howling Host”

  • HP: 12 × players
  • Attack: Each player loses 2 life
  • Chaos: Create a 2/2 Spirit
  • Reward: Exile a card from a graveyard; if it’s a creature, create a 2/2 Zombie
  • Penalty: “At each upkeep, mill 2.”

🏆 Final Plane

The last plane contains a two-phase boss.

Example — New Phyrexia

  • Phase 1: Tap creatures, sacrifice permanents
  • Phase 2: Cumulative poison and doubled damage

Players who damage the final boss receive a legendary emblem.
